# Anno 1644 - Flag definitions for the colonial independence nations
# Companion to coat_of_arms/coat_of_arms/w_colonial_coa.txt
#
# priority values above 10000 are needed to override the default random definitions.
# The subject definition re-uses the national coat of arms and adds the overlord's
# canton while the country is still a colonial subject.
