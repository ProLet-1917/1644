# Anno 1644 - Colonial nations in the Age of Imperialism
#
# Generic advances for colonial subjects and former colonies, plus national branches
# for the great New World nations.
#
# Binding rules:
#  * Generic colonial advances require the colonial government reform
#    (government_reform:colonial_government_reform, defined in 0_5_reforms.txt).
#  * National advances are bound to their country tag (USA / NSP / NFR / CAN) where such
#    a tag exists. Brazil and the Rio de la Plata have no dedicated tag in this scenario,
#    so their branches are bound to the region their capital lies in.
